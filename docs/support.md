# Kinledger Help

## Common questions

**Is my log safe if I lose my phone?**
Yes, if cloud backup is on (Me → Backup). Sign in with the same Apple account on the new phone and choose Restore on the first screen. Without backup, your entries are only on the phone, so turn it on or export regularly.

**I deleted an entry by mistake.**
Open Me → Trash. Entries stay there for 30 days.

**I changed an entry and want the old version back.**
Open the entry and scroll to History. Tap a version to bring it back.

**Does my family need the app?**
No. They get the monthly report as a PDF or a private link that opens in any browser.

**Are my notes in the report?**
Only if you switch on "Include my private notes" before sharing.

**How do I cancel Pro?**
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kinledger. Your entries and exports stay available after cancelling.

**Can I get all my data out?**
Me → Export all my data: a spreadsheet plus every receipt photo, free at any time.
