# Kinledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Kinledger is a care journal: you log the time, money and visits you give to a parent or relative, and send a monthly report to your family. This policy explains what happens to that information.

## The short version

- Your entries are yours. We do not sell them, show ads, or use them to train anything.
- Without cloud backup, everything stays on your iPhone.
- With cloud backup, a copy is stored so you can get it back on a new phone. Only you can read it.
- You can export everything, or delete your cloud copy and account, at any time from the app.

## What Kinledger stores

| What | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entries: category, time, amounts, who paid, notes, dates | On your iPhone; in the cloud if you turn on backup | To keep your log and build reports |
| Receipt photos you take or choose | On your iPhone; in the cloud if you turn on backup | To attach proof to expenses |
| Who you care for (name, what you call them, country) and the family members you add to the report (name, email or phone) | On your iPhone; in the cloud if you turn on backup | To address and send the report |
| An account identifier from Sign in with Apple | In the cloud, only if you turn on backup | To know which copy is yours |

Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email. Kinledger asks Apple for no name or email at all.

## What Kinledger does not do

- No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
- No selling or renting of data.
- No medical advice. Kinledger records what you enter and nothing more.

## Cloud backup

Backup is off until you sign in with Apple in the app. The copy is stored with Supabase (database and file storage) on servers in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. Every record is locked to your account: other users, including family members, cannot read it. Your family sees only a report you choose to send.

## Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Kinledger never sees your card details.

## Your choices

- **Export:** Me → Export all my data gives you a spreadsheet of every entry plus all receipt photos, on the free plan and after a subscription ends.
- **Delete:** deleting an entry moves it to the trash for 30 days, then it is removed. Me → Delete cloud account removes your cloud copy and account; entries on your iPhone stay until you delete the app.
- **Questions or requests:** see the [support page](support).

## Children

Kinledger is meant for adults caring for a family member and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and important changes will be shown in the app.
