# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect → version → App Review Information → Notes. No demo account is needed.

---

Kinledger is a care journal for adults looking after a parent. People log the time, money and visits they give, and once a month share a summary with siblings.

HOW TO TRY IT
1. Open the app, enter any name on the first screen and tap Next, then "Start logging" (or "I'll add them later").
2. Tap + to log something: pick a category, time, an amount and a receipt photo, then Save.
3. Today shows the month's totals. Log lists every entry. Swipe an entry to delete it; Me → Trash brings it back.

No sign-in is needed for any of that. Everything is stored on the iPhone.

CLOUD BACKUP (OPTIONAL)
In Me, "Continue with Apple" (Sign in with Apple) turns on a backup in our Supabase database, so entries come back on a new phone.
Account deletion: Me → Delete cloud account, confirmed with Apple. It removes the cloud copy and the account.

KINLEDGER PRO (AUTO-RENEWABLE SUBSCRIPTION)
Monthly and yearly, both with a 7-day free trial. Pro unlocks:
- the monthly family report as a PDF, or as a private web link that relatives open in a browser with no app or account (the link needs backup turned on; Me can turn all links off);
- the US medical-expense export (a spreadsheet and the receipt photos) for a tax preparer. It is a record of payments, not tax advice.
To see the paywall, open Report and tap "Share PDF with Pro". Restore is in the paywall's top bar.
Logging, totals, receipts, history, backup and full data export stay free, also after a subscription ends.

NOTIFICATIONS
After the first report is shared, the app asks once whether to remind on the 1st of the month. The reminder only opens the report; nothing is sent automatically.

The app does not give medical advice and does not use HealthKit.
