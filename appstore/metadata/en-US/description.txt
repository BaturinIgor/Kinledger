Caring for a parent is a lot of quiet work. Kinledger keeps a record of it.

Log a pharmacy run, a doctor visit, an overnight stay or a grocery bill in a few taps: how long it took, what you paid, and a photo of the receipt. Kinledger adds it up.

ONE CLEAR REPORT FOR THE FAMILY
Once a month, share a calm summary with your brothers and sisters: hours of care, visits, money paid out of pocket, and every entry with the time it was logged. They open it as a PDF or a private link. They don't need the app or an account, and you can turn the link off at any time.

AN HONEST RECORD
Every entry shows when it was logged. Changes keep their history, and deleted entries wait in Trash for 30 days. Nothing is lost by accident.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your log stays on your iPhone. Turn on cloud backup with Sign in with Apple to get it back on a new phone. No ads, no tracking, and your entries are never sold.

FOR TAX TIME
In the United States, export the medical expenses you paid for the year, with receipts, ready for your tax preparer.

FREE: logging, totals, receipts, history, backup and full export of your data.
KINLEDGER PRO: the monthly family report and the tax export, monthly or yearly. Cancel any time in iPhone Settings.
